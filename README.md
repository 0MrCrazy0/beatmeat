# BEAT MEAT: Bring a Friend. Leave a Mess. (v15.0.1)

An original, gory 2D fighter and co-op street brawler. Play it free in your browser, on desktop or phone, and install it as an app.

**Play now: https://0mrcrazy0.github.io/beatmeat/**

## Modes
- **Versus / Arcade / Survival / Training**: 11 fighters, four-limb controls, strings, specials and hidden fatalities.
- **Story**: two co-op campaigns. *Blood Vendetta* (Ray and Dane) and *Take Back the City* (Jax, Rena, Brick, Kenji and Rivet the courier). Grab bats, pipes, blades, bricks, bottles, dynamite and barrels, and knock weapons out of enemy hands. Six new gang types, team slams and a two-player DOUBLE STRIKE.
- **Online**: host a match, send your friend the link or the 5-letter code, and play Versus or Story co-op. Each player picks their own fighter.

## Controls (Xbox pad / keyboard)
**Versus & Online**: each button is one limb. New in v15: a 10-hit string, left and right throws (with escapes), back-turned stance and crouch-dash.
X / U = 1 left punch, Y / I = 2 right punch, A / J = 3 left kick, B / K = 4 right kick.
1+2 = throw, 3+4 = signature move, hold back = block. Full move list in PAUSE.

**Blood Vendetta**: X punch, A kick, B or Y jump, LB or RB guard, guard + X special, X + A back elbow. Finish a dazed boss with X. Prefer the arcade feel? Turn on **Settings > Controls > CLASSIC DD BUTTONS** (left attack / right attack, both = jump).

**Take Back the City**: X attack, A jump, Y special, forward + Y rush, X + A back attack, forward forward + X blitz, LB police call. Walk into a thug to grab, then A to vault or X to suplex. **SOR 6-BUTTON** in Settings > Controls puts back attack on B. Hold a thug while your partner hits it for a TEAM SLAM; both press special together for a DOUBLE STRIKE.

On touch screens every mode gets its own buttons, plus a RUN button in Story. Every menu works with just a mouse or a finger: click or tap a setting's left half to lower it and right half to raise it, click tabs, scroll with the wheel, and right-click or tap BACK to go back. Player 2 keyboard keys can be remapped in Settings.

## Playing online
1. ONLINE > HOST, then tap **COPY LINK** (or SHARE) and send it.
2. Your friend opens the link and joins automatically. They can also choose ONLINE > JOIN and type the code.
3. Both players pick and press READY. You can show or hide the ping meter in Settings > Online.
