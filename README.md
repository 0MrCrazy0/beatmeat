# SPLATTER CIRCUIT
### *Eleven fighters. One bloody Circuit. No mercy.*

Splatter Circuit is a fast, gory, six-button arcade fighting game with a co-op street-brawler story mode. Pick from eleven fighters, each built on a real martial art, and fight your way through arcade ladders, head-to-head versus battles and two story campaigns. Land combos, fill your meter, unleash bone-cracking X-Rays and finish your opponent when the announcer screams **END THEM!**

It runs in your browser on PC, TV, tablet and phone, and works with an Xbox-style controller, a keyboard or touch.

---

## How to play
- **Play online:** open the game's GitHub Pages link in any modern browser.
- **Play from a download:** open `index.html` in a browser.
- **Install it as an app:**
  - **Phone (Android / Chrome):** open the game, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.
  - **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
  - **PC (Chrome / Edge):** click the install icon in the address bar, or menu > **Install Splatter Circuit**.
  - Once installed, it launches full-screen and plays offline.
- Plug in a controller at any time. Button prompts switch to match the device you are using.

## Game modes
| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Arcade** | Climb the Circuit ladder, fight every challenger and face the boss at the top. Each fighter has their own ending. |
| **Versus** | 1P vs 2P or vs CPU, with stage select. |
| **Story** | Side-scrolling street brawler for 1 or 2 players in co-op. Player 2 can join at any time by pressing START. |
| **Survival** | How many fighters can you beat on one health bar? |
| **Training** | Practise freely with dummy settings, hitboxes, input display and meter fill. |
| **Tutorial** | Step-by-step lessons on the basics. **How to Play** shows every control. |
| **Extras** | Gallery, achievements, stats, leaderboards and unlockables. |

### Story campaigns
**Story 1: TAKE BACK THE CITY** (8 stages)
> The Malgrave crime family owns the mayor, the courts and the police. Now it wants the streets too. Fight district by district, beat each of the Family's lieutenants, and climb all the way to the top. Call in the **riot van** once per life when things get ugly.

**Story 2: BLOOD VENDETTA** (6 missions)
> Saint Kane's crew blows open your garage and kidnaps Nova, your partner and best friend. Chase them across the city through rooftops, scrapyards and speeding trains, past pits, spikes and live rails, and get her back before sunrise.

Both stories have lives, continues, score, a leaderboard and auto-save so you can **CONTINUE** later. Each has two endings. Beat the last two stages without using a continue to see the true one.

## Controls
The same controls work in every mode, including Story.

### Xbox controller
| Action | Button |
|---|---|
| Light / Medium / Heavy Punch | **X** / **Y** / **RB** |
| Light / Medium / Heavy Kick | **A** / **B** / **RT** |
| Throw / Grab | **LB** |
| Meter (EX / Super / X-Ray) | **LT** |
| Move / Jump / Crouch | Left stick or D-pad |
| Block | Hold away from the opponent (down-back blocks low) |
| Dash / Run | Tap forward twice (keep holding to run) |
| Pause | **MENU** |
| Back | **VIEW** |

PlayStation and Switch controllers work too, and the on-screen prompts show their buttons.

### Keyboard
| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Move / Jump / Crouch | **W A S D** | **Arrow keys** |
| Light / Medium / Heavy Punch | **U** / **I** / **O** | **Num 7** / **Num 8** / **Num 9** |
| Light / Medium / Heavy Kick | **J** / **K** / **L** | **Num 4** / **Num 5** / **Num 6** |
| Throw / Grab | **H** | **Num 1** |
| Meter | **Space** | **Num 0** |
| Block button (when BLOCK BUTTON is on in Settings) | **Q** | **Num 3** |
| Run | **E** | **Num 2** |
| Pause | **Enter** | **Num Enter** |
| Back | **Esc** | **Num +** |

The two layouts share no keys, so two players can share one keyboard. All keys can be rebound in **Settings > Keyboard**.

### Touch
| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Joystick** (left side) | Move, jump, crouch, block (hold away) |
| **LP MP HP** (top row) | Light / Medium / Heavy Punch |
| **LK MK HK** (bottom row) | Light / Medium / Heavy Kick |
| **GRAB** | Throw or grab. In Story it turns gold and reads **THROW** when you are holding a weapon. |
| **METER** | EX, Super and X-Ray |
| **BLOCK** / **RUN** | Block and run buttons |
| **II** (top of the screen) | Pause |

Move and resize any button in **Settings > Edit Touch Layout**. There is also a **SIMPLE** layout with one punch, one kick, special and throw.

## Basics
- **Six buttons:** light attacks are fast, heavy attacks are slow and hit hard, and medium attacks are in between.
- **Block:** hold away from your opponent. Hold down-back to block low attacks. Overheads must be blocked standing.
- **Dash and run:** tap forward twice to dash, and keep holding to run. Tap back twice to back-dash.
- **Throw:** press Throw/Grab up close.
- **Specials:** a motion plus a punch (**P**) or a kick (**K**). The strength you press changes the speed, distance and damage. Turn on **Settings > EASY SPECIALS** to fire specials with **Meter + direction + button** instead.
- **Meter (3 bars)** fills as you fight:
  - **EX** (1 bar): hold **Meter** (or press two punches / two kicks) while doing a special for a powered-up version.
  - **Super** (2 bars): **↓↘→↓↘→ + P**, or **Meter + HP**.
  - **X-Ray** (3 bars): **↓↘→↓↘→ + K**, or **Meter + HK**. A slow-motion, bone-breaking cinematic.
- **Combo Breaker** (1 bar): press **Meter + Throw** while you are being hit to break out of a combo.
- **Parry:** tap forward just before an attack lands (tap down for low attacks) to deflect it.
- **Combos:** chain normals into each other and cancel them into specials. Each fighter has target combos, listed in the **Move List**.
- **Finishers:** when **END THEM!** appears, enter one of your fighter's finisher inputs (see the Move List) to finish your opponent in style. Some arenas also have a stage kill.
- **Kill-cam:** the deciding blow is replayed in slow motion. Press any button to skip it, or turn it off in **Settings > Video > KO REPLAY**.

### Story moves
- **Weapons:** walk over a pipe, bat, knife or chain and press **LP** to pick it up. Attack to swing it. Weapons break after enough hits, and you drop them when you are knocked down.
- **Throw a weapon:** press **Throw/Grab** while holding one. It flies, hits, and can be picked up again.
- **Hair grab:** press **Throw/Grab** next to a dazed thug. Then press **LP** for knees or **LK / HK** to throw them (hold back to throw them behind you).
- **Back elbow:** **← + HP** hits an enemy behind you.
- **Change lanes:** hold **↑** or **↓**. Tap **↑** to jump.
- **Star move:** **Meter + HP**.
- **Riot van** (Take Back the City): **Meter + Throw** calls backup once per life.
- Mash buttons to escape bear hugs.

### Notation
**↓↘→** quarter circle forward, **↓↙←** quarter circle back, **→↓↘** dragon punch, **←↓↙** reverse dragon punch, **←↙↓↘→** half circle forward, **→↘↓↙←** half circle back, **[←] →** hold back about a second then press forward, **[↓] ↑** hold down then press up, **360°** full circle, **RAPID** press that button type quickly 3 times. All motions assume you are facing right.

## Tips
- **Block first, punish second.** Heavy attacks and big specials leave fighters open when they miss or get blocked.
- **Mix high and low.** Crouching attacks beat standing blocks, and overheads beat crouching blocks.
- **Don't sit on full meter.** One EX special can turn a round around. Save 3 bars for an X-Ray when you need a big swing.
- **Keep a bar for Combo Breaker** against fighters who combo hard.
- **Learn your fighter's game plan.** Okafor wants distance, Magma Mae and Tusk want to grab you, and Razor and Kira want to stay in your face.
- **Story mode:** grab weapons early, throw knives at enemies that keep their distance, and use lane changes to stop thugs surrounding you. In co-op, split up and take a side each.
- **Want the true endings?** Save your continues for the last two stages.
